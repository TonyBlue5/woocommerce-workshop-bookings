Short description

Sell workshop sessions and monthly participation through WooCommerce, manage shared capacity, and bring customers back with loyalty rewards, referrals and timely messages.

Staff calendar and phone bookings

See participants and availability in the WooCommerce admin calendar. Administrators and Shop Managers can record phone bookings, release one meeting from a monthly booking, and reserve it again if capacity allows. Online and offline reservations share the same places.

Full description

Keep the checkout your customers already know. Workshop Bookings Pro adds visual workshop availability, recurring schedules, multi-month purchases and shared capacity to WooCommerce products.

Understand your business with workshop revenue reports and lapsed-customer lists. Reward returning customers with targeted coupons, and let customers invite friends through personal referral links. Qualifying guest purchases count by billing email, with signed attribution and duplicate protections.

Send attendance reminders, notify participants about cancellations, and reuse your own rich-text message templates. Customers find their bookings, calendar exports and earned rewards in My Account.

Requirements and scope

Requires WordPress, WooCommerce, a working mail transport and cron runner. Monthly bookings are prepaid purchases, not automatic subscription billing. Calendar exports are not live synchronization. Affiliate-style rewards are discount coupons, not cash payouts. Validate your target theme and gateway before going live.

Included

Installable Pro plugin, offline HTML documentation, changelog and license. Customer data and third-party images are not included.

FAQ

Do I need WooCommerce? Yes.

Can I sell single and monthly bookings? Yes; enable either or both per product.

Are monthly purchases automatically renewed? No; customers purchase selected months.

Do guest referrals count? Yes, for eligible completed paid orders with valid attribution.

Does Google Calendar need an API key? No.

Can I cancel one workshop date? Pro can close the date and notify its participants. Refunds remain a separate WooCommerce action.

Does it work for adults? Yes. It is designed for classes, studios, workshops and activities for any age group.

Can I upgrade from Lite without losing bookings? Yes. Back up, deactivate Lite and activate Pro.
